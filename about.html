A one-bay shop on Hill Ave that does it right

High Altitude Auto Spa is owner-run by Ryan Clark. He's been tinting and detailing for more than five years, and he's the one who answers the phone, quotes the job, and does the work.

How we work

  • You get the price before we start, and it's the price you pay. No add-ons at pickup.
  • Work happens indoors. Tint and coatings need a clean, lit bay to go on right. A driveway isn't one.
  • Ceramic film and real ceramic coatings only. Ryan is a Nasiol Pro Club certified installer.
  • We'll tell you what you don't need. Half the cars that come in for a coating need a good correction more.
