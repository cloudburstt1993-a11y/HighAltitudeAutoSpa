Why it matters here

What the Grand Valley does to a car, and how we undo it

Red dust and grit

Fine desert dust gets past door seals and into vents, seat seams, and carpet. We blow it out with compressed air and extract it, instead of vacuuming the top layer.

Winter mag chloride

The deicer on I-70 and the mountain passes clings to paint, wheels, and the underbody, and holds moisture against metal. We dissolve it off instead of rinsing over it.

High-desert sun

Strong sun at elevation fades paint, dries out leather, and cracks dashboards. We correct the damage and finish with UV protection.

Done indoors, out of the wind

Detailing in a driveway means blowing dust landing on wet paint and sun baking products onto hot panels. In our shop, your vehicle gets steady lighting, controlled conditions, and time for products to cure properly.
