Pick a shade

Most people land on 20% on the back and 35% up front. Try a few.

The number is how much light gets through. Colorado lets you run 27% or darker on the back half as long as the front doors stay at 70%, and a visor strip across the top of the windshield. We'll tell you what's legal on your vehicle before any film goes on.

What the Grand Valley does to a car

Sun at 4,600 feet

Thinner air, stronger UV. It fades paint, cooks dashboards, and turns a parked car into an oven. Ceramic film cuts the heat and blocks UV; a coating keeps the paint from chalking.

Red dust

It gets past door seals and into vents, seams, and carpet. We blow it out with compressed air and extract it instead of vacuuming the top layer.

Winter mag chloride

The deicer on I-70 and the Monument road clings to paint and wheels and holds moisture against metal. We dissolve it off instead of rinsing over it.
