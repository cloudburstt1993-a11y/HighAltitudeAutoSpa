Ceramic coating in Grand Junction

A hard, slick layer bonded to your paint: deeper gloss, easier washing, and a shield against UV, mag chloride, and bug and bird etching. Applied by a Nasiol-certified installer.

Priced by vehicle size and condition. Call for a number.

What's included

  • Full wash and decontamination
  • Paint polished or corrected so the coating locks in a clean finish, not swirl marks
  • Panel wipe to strip oils so the coating bonds to the clear coat
  • Nasiol coating applied panel by panel, indoors, under good light
  • Cure time in the shop, out of dust and weather
  • Aftercare: how to wash it so it lasts, and a maintenance plan if you want one

Why it matters here

Coatings earn their keep in the Grand Valley: UV that fades paint, mag chloride that clings all winter, and desert dust that rinses off a slick surface instead of grinding in. A coated car is also faster to wash, which matters when the dust is back the next day.

Certified Nasiol installer

Ryan is a Nasiol Pro Club certified installer, which means the coatings we apply are the real product, prepped and cured the way the manufacturer specifies, and backed by them. Ask to see the certificate; it's on the wall.

What a coating is

A liquid nano-ceramic that bonds to the clear coat and hardens into a glass-like layer. Harder than wax, far longer lasting than a sealant. Water and dirt slide off, UV is blocked, and the gloss stays.
