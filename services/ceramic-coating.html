Why it matters in the Grand Valley

Coatings earn their keep here because of what they protect against: UV that fades paint, mag chloride that clings all winter, and desert dust that rinses off a slick surface instead of grinding in.

A coated vehicle is also faster to wash, which matters when the dust is back the next day.

How long does a coating last?

It depends on the coating package and how the vehicle is cared for afterward. We'll walk you through the options and what each one is rated for, so you can pick the one that fits how long you plan to keep the car.
