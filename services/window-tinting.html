Window tinting in Grand Junction

Ceramic window film, computer-cut to your vehicle and laid in the shop, out of the dust. Cooler cabin, less glare, 99% of UV blocked, and a cleaner look.

What's included

  • Film pattern cut by machine for your exact year, make, and model
  • Ceramic film in your choice of shade, with the legal limits explained up front
  • Old or failing tint stripped and glass cleaned, if you're replacing film
  • Installed indoors in a clean bay so nothing gets trapped under the film
  • Edges shrunk and tucked, no gaps, no light lines
  • Care instructions: leave the windows up a few days while it cures

Why tint here

Grand Junction gets about 300 sunny days a year at 4,600 feet. A tinted car sits cooler in the lot, the dash and seats last longer, and you're not squinting on the drive home. On an SUV with kids in the back, it's the cheapest comfort upgrade there is.

Pick a shade

Most people land on 20% on the back and 35% up front. Try a few.

The number is how much light gets through. Colorado lets you run 27% or darker on the back half as long as the front doors stay at 70%, and a visor strip across the top of the windshield. We'll tell you what's legal on your vehicle before any film goes on.

20% — the dark look most people picture when they say "tinted."

Ceramic film, not dyed film

Cheap dyed tint turns purple and bubbles after a couple of Grand Junction summers. Ceramic film holds its color, rejects far more heat at the same shade, and doesn't interfere with phone or GPS signal. It costs more up front and it's the only kind we put on.

Windshields too

A light ceramic film on the windshield (50% or 70%) is nearly invisible and takes the sting out of a west-facing commute on I-70B. We'll check what's allowed on your vehicle first.
