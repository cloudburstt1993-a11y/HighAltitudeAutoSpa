Paint correction in Grand Junction

Machine polishing that removes swirl marks, light scratches, and sun oxidation, so your paint reflects again instead of looking hazy.

What's included

  • Full wash and decontamination, so nothing scratches the paint during polishing
  • Paint inspection under shop lighting to find every swirl and defect
  • Trim and rubber masked off to protect them from the polisher
  • Single- or multi-stage machine polishing, matched to the condition of your paint
  • Panel wipe to check the true finish, with no fillers hiding defects
  • Protection applied afterward: sealant, or ceramic coating if you book it

Why it matters in the Grand Valley

Hard sun at elevation oxidizes clear coat, especially on dark colors and older vehicles. Years of brush car washes add fine swirls that show up every time the sun hits the paint. Correction levels the clear coat to take them out.

It's also the right first step before a ceramic coating. A coating locks in whatever is underneath it, swirls included.

What polishing can and can't fix

Scratches deep enough to catch a fingernail are usually through the clear coat and need paint work, not polishing. We'll look at your vehicle and tell you what correction will fix before you commit.

Pairs well with

Ceramic coating

A hard, slick layer bonded to your paint: deeper gloss, easier washing, and a shield against UV, mag chloride, and bug and bird etching.

Exterior detailing

A careful hand wash, full decontamination, and a layer of protection between your paint and the weather.
